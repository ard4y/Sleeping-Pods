Who we are

A bed for the gap in your day.

We place private, soundproof sleep capsules on university campuses. Book one in the app and take a science-backed 40-minute nap between classes. No commute home, no bench, no noise.

What we are

A bookable nap capsule on campus: quiet, private and ready every time.

Why we exist

Students wait hours between classes with nowhere to rest. We want rest to be a normal, easy part of the student day.

Whose problem we solve

Students with long gaps between classes, starting with commuters.

    The science

    Why forty minutes?

    A short nap can lift alertness without leaving you in a heavy sleep. Here is what the research says, and what we still have to test.

    25.8minutes asleep on average in a 40-minute rest window

    NASA's 40-minute window

    Pilots given a 40-minute rest window fell asleep in 5.6 minutes and slept 25.8 minutes on average. They showed 54% higher alertness and 34% better job performance than pilots with no rest.

    Rosekind et al. (1995) · 21 crew members, so supporting evidence, not proof for campuses

    Cooling helps you drop off

    Falling asleep is closely tied to how the body regulates temperature, so the capsule cools gently as you settle.

    Harding, Franks and Wisden (2019)

    Naps help thinking

    A systematic review found that afternoon naps improve cognitive performance, mostly in laboratory studies.

    Dutheil et al. (2021)

    What we are building on

    • A short nap has the most support, so a session is 40 minutes with a gentle wake-up.
    • Lavender aroma showed a sleep benefit in a meta-analysis (Shen, Zhang and Zhu, 2025).
    • Rising light, a soft alarm and a cushion pulse are meant to reduce grogginess.

    What we still have to test

    • Lavender researchers call for more high-quality trials, so scent is optional.
    • Evidence that continuous sound helps sleep is limited (Riedy et al., 2021), so sound is always your choice.
    • In one laboratory study, grogginess appeared only after 30–60 minute naps, was small and cleared within 30 minutes (NUS Medicine, 2023). We will measure ours in a pilot.

    Our impact

    Built around three global goals.

    Rest is part of learning and health. The capsule is also a new kind of campus infrastructure. We link the project to three of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

    3SDG 3

    Good Health and Well-being

    Students get rest in the middle of the day instead of pushing through exhaustion. Short naps have been linked to higher alertness (Rosekind et al., 1995).

    What we will measureBooked sessions, and students' own sleepiness ratings before and after a nap in a pilot.
    4SDG 4

    Quality Education

    Alert students can concentrate better in their next class. A systematic review links daytime naps to better cognitive performance (Dutheil et al., 2021). Commuters get a place to recharge on campus.

    What we will measureStudents' self-rated focus after a nap, and use by commuters and students with long gaps.
    9SDG 9

    Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

    A new campus service built from app booking, automated cleaning and smart climate control. It is a student-led innovation that adds useful infrastructure to a university.

    What we will measureCapsule availability, cleaning cycles completed and booking data kept to a minimum.

    The measures above are what we plan to track in a pilot, not results.
